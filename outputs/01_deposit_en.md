### Understanding your situation
You are a tenant who vacated a flat in Pune on 15 August 2026 and handed over the keys. Your landlord is withholding your ₹40,000 security deposit without giving any reason, despite there being no damage and no pending rent.

### Where you stand legally
- Your landlord is required to refund the security deposit at the time of taking over vacant possession, after making due deductions for any liability, if the state has enacted a law based on the Model Tenancy Act. [1]
- At the end of the lease, you are required to hand back possession of the property to the landlord. [2]
- You have a general limitation period of three years from when the right to sue accrues to file a civil suit to recover money such as an unreturned security deposit. [3]

### Your options
1. **Civil Suit for Money Recovery** — File a civil suit in the appropriate civil court to recover the ₹40,000 security deposit based on the three-year limitation period. [3]

### Time limits
- You have a limitation period of three years from when the right to sue accrues to file a civil suit for recovering the unreturned security deposit. [3]

### What to do next
1. Send a written demand to your landlord asking for the return of your ₹40,000 security deposit.
2. Organize your rent agreement and bank transfer proof of the deposit.
3. Consult a local advocate in Pune to discuss filing a civil money recovery suit if the landlord continues to withhold the amount.
