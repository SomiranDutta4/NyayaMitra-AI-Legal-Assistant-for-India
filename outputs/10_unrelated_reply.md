### Understanding your situation
You built a website for a startup in Mumbai under an agreement formed via email quote and WhatsApp messages. The client failed to pay the ₹60,000 due on 15 September 2026, which is a breach of contract.

### Where you stand legally
- Your agreement formed through WhatsApp messages and an email quote constitutes a valid contract, as contracts do not strictly require a formal signed document unless a law specifies otherwise. [1]
- Because the client failed to pay the ₹60,000 due on 15 September 2026, you are entitled to claim compensation for the financial loss that naturally arose from this breach. [2]
- Since your agreement does not contain an arbitration clause, the requirement to refer disputes to arbitration does not apply, and you may approach the regular civil courts. [3]

### Your options
1. **Civil Suit for Compensation** — File a suit in a civil court to recover the ₹60,000 contract amount plus compensation for the breach. [2][4]

### Time limits
- You must file a suit for compensation for breach of contract within three years from the date when the contract was broken (15 September 2026). [4]

### What to do next
