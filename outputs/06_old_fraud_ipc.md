### Understanding your situation
In March 2024 in Lucknow, a man took ₹2,00,000 from you promising a government job and disappeared. You have not yet filed an FIR, but you possess UPI screenshots and his phone number as evidence.

### Where you stand legally
- Because the incident occurred in March 2024, which is before 1 July 2024, your case falls under the Indian Penal Code, 1860 for cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property. [1]
- The offence of cheating and dishonestly inducing delivery of property carries a punishment of imprisonment up to seven years and a fine. [1]
- You have the right to report information about this cognizable offence to the officer in charge of a police station to have an FIR recorded, and you must be given a free copy. [2]

### Your options
1. **Police Complaint / FIR** — Go to the local police station in Lucknow to report the crime under Section 154 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, for an official investigation into the cheating. [2][1]
2. **Superintendent of Police Complaint** — If the police station officer refuses to record your information, you may send the information in writing to the Superintendent of Police. [2]

### What to do next
1. Prepare a written complaint detailing how the man took ₹2,00,000 in March 2024 promising a government job and then disappeared.
2. Print out your UPI screenshots and note down his phone number clearly.
3. Visit the local police station in Lucknow to file your FIR under Section 154.
4. Obtain and keep safe a free copy of the recorded FIR from the police officer.
5. If the police station refuses to record your complaint, send a written complaint to the Superintendent of Police.
