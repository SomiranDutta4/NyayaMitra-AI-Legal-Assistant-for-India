### Understanding your situation
You are currently staying safely at your sister's house after leaving your husband last week due to physical abuse and threats to kill you. You want legal protection and financial support for yourself and your son.

### Where you stand legally
- Your husband's acts of physical abuse and threats to your life constitute domestic violence, which allows you to seek protection and monetary relief from a Magistrate. [1][2]
- Subjecting a wife to cruelty, including physical abuse and grave danger to her life, is a criminal offence punishable with imprisonment up to three years and fine under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. [3]
- Threatening you with death constitutes criminal intimidation, which is punishable under the law with imprisonment up to seven years, or fine, or both. [4]

### Your options
1. **Domestic Violence Application** — Apply to the Magistrate for protection orders to stop your husband from committing violence or contacting you, and monetary relief orders covering expenses and maintenance for you and your child. [5][2]
2. **Police Complaint / FIR** — Provide information about the cognizable offences of cruelty and criminal intimidation to the police station to record your complaint and receive a free copy. [6]
3. **Hindu Marriage Act Maintenance** — If court proceedings are initiated under the Hindu Marriage Act, you can ask the court to order your husband to pay a monthly sum and litigation expenses during the case if you have no independent income sufficient for your support. [7][8]

### Time limits
- The first hearing for a domestic violence application is ordinarily fixed within three days of the Magistrate receiving the application, and the Magistrate should try to dispose of it within sixty days of the first hearing. [5]

### What to do next
1. Go to the nearest police station to report the physical abuse and threats to your life, and obtain a free copy of the recorded information.
2. Consult a lawyer or a Protection Officer to file an application before the Magistrate for protection and monetary relief.
3. Gather and secure any medical reports, photographs, or text messages related to the abuse.
4. Prepare details of your and your son's financial needs for your support application.

### Keep these ready
- Medical reports or prescriptions if you sought treatment for injuries
- Photographs of any injuries
- Chat messages, call logs, or recordings showing threats or abuse
- Marriage certificate or wedding photographs as proof of marriage
